# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants in the Atmanatic Research project are expected to make technical
discussion useful, honest, and respectful. Welcome careful disagreement,
question assumptions, distinguish evidence from inference, and explain
tradeoffs without attacking people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing,
sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, impersonation, publishing private
information, or bad-faith manipulation of review, evidence, or governance
processes.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner on GitHub. Do
not use a public issue for sensitive reports. Include links, dates, context,
and any requested confidentiality. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and
confidentially as practical.

Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, close discussions, or
take other proportionate action. Decisions should be grounded in the project
rules and applied consistently.
